Capability

Equipment

The platforms and sensors we fly, drive, and deploy across research plots and working fields.

Robots

  • farm-ng Amiga

    Modular autonomous ground robot used as the lab's field platform for weed detection, sensor carriage, and under-canopy data collection.

UAVs

  • Multirotor UAVs ×3

    Survey aircraft for multispectral and RGB capture across research plots.

  • Quadcopter

    Smaller airframe for low-altitude and close-range capture.

  • WingtraOne GEN II

    Fixed-wing VTOL mapping aircraft. It takes off and lands vertically like a multirotor, then flies as a fixed wing to cover large fields efficiently.

Sensors

  • RGB camera sensors ×2

    Visible-spectrum imaging for high-resolution crop and weed assessment.

  • Multispectral camera sensors ×2

    Multi-band capture supporting vegetation indices and crop stress detection.

  • Wingtra RGB61

    61-megapixel full-frame RGB camera for the Wingtra aircraft, capturing high-resolution imagery for mapping and for crop and weed assessment.

This list is growing. If you're interested in collaborating with us, reach out!
