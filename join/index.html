Join Us

We work with motivated students and collaborators at the intersection of precision agriculture, AI, remote sensing, and robotics.

Prospective graduate students

We recruit through the departments of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering and Computer Science at NDSU. Backgrounds in remote sensing, computer vision, agronomy, robotics, or data science are all a good fit — prior agriculture experience is not required.

When you email, include a short note on what you want to work on, your CV, and your transcript.

Undergraduate researchers

NDSU undergraduates interested in hands-on research with field data, drones, or machine learning are encouraged to get in touch. Semester and summer positions are available.

Collaborators & partners

We welcome collaboration with researchers, industry partners, and growers working on field-scale agricultural problems.
