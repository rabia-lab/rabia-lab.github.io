Graduate Students

  • Mohamed Abdallah Salem

    Graduate Student · ABEN

    Mohamed brings a background in mechatronics, artificial intelligence, and control systems to agricultural problems, drawn to the lab for its interdisciplinary approach. His work centres on moving AI out of the laboratory and into practical, real-time field use.

    Research focus Robotics, ML, DL, Mechatronics

    Currently working on
    • Edge AI systems for real-time crop and weed detection
    • An agricultural AI model zoo for low-power hardware
    • Robotic laser-based weed control
    • Low-cost wireless livestock tracking using UWB and digital twins
    • Computer vision and remote sensing from ground and UAV imagery
    • Sensing and control for controlled-environment agriculture
    GitHub

  • Prakash Chapagain

    Graduate Student · ABEN

    Prakash holds an undergraduate degree in Agricultural Engineering from IOE Purwanchal Engineering Campus, Nepal, where a focus on mechanization grew into a passion for automation, UAVs, and precision agriculture. His research turns UAV imagery into actionable data for plant health assessment and weed detection, aiming to replace blanket herbicide application with site-specific management that saves growers money and protects soil health.

    Research focus Agronomy, Remote Sensing, GIS

    Currently working on
    • Assessing weed canopy cover in organic high-density and row crops from UAV data
    • Comparing deep learning models, RGB vegetation indices, and NDVI for weed detection
    • Building crop yield stability maps to drive variable-rate seeding
    GitHub
  • Y

    Yoko (George) Kosugi

    Graduate Student · ABEN

    George’s interest in the geosciences led to an undergraduate degree in geology, where a GIS class sparked a passion for geospatial science, and later to a master’s degree in Natural Resource Sciences at NDSU with hands-on experience in precision agriculture. George joined the lab when Dr. Rabia was looking for someone who could handle drones and GIS. Today, George collects high-resolution data by flying UAVs over crop fields and analyzes it with GIS, Python, machine learning, deep learning, and AI.

    Agronomy, Remote Sensing, GIS

  • Mamoona Nisar

  • Apekshya Dhakal

    Apekshya joined the lab to build on a background in agriculture and explore how UAS, remote sensing, and advanced geospatial technologies can be used in precision agriculture. Research interests center on combining these technologies with data-driven approaches to address agricultural challenges, with the goal of supporting more efficient and sustainable practices.

Undergraduate Researchers

  • Georgina Sieh

    Undergraduate Researcher · Data Science

    Georgina joined the lab to build skills in AI and machine learning, and found a supportive, team-minded environment with room to work independently.
